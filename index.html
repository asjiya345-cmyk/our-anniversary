To Suryansh,

Happy anniversary, Suryansh. Four years. 1,461 days since 9 November 2022 — and not a single second has gone by without me thinking about you at least once. Honestly, that's a lot of time to have someone on your mind.

I know we're in a different phase of life right now. We have studies to focus on, careers to build, and a lot of things we want to achieve for ourselves. There will be plenty to figure out, and I know we both have to work hard to get where we want to be.
